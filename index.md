# Tower and Crown

A fantasy tower defense game for iPhone, iPad and Mac. No ads, no purchases, and nothing collected about you.

## Support

Something not working, or an idea to share? [Write to us here](https://github.com/Dealer-Of-Happiness/tower-and-crown-site/issues/new) (a free GitHub account is needed). Say what happened, on which device, and which version of the game (it is at the bottom of Settings).

## Common questions

**My progress is not on my other device.** Both devices need to be signed in to the same iCloud account, and "Keep progress in iCloud" has to be on in the game's Settings on both. It can take a few minutes to arrive.

**The leaderboards say I am signed out.** Sign in to Game Center in the Settings app of your device. Your own best scores are kept in the game either way.

**How do I play with a keyboard or a controller?** Connect it, pause a battle, and open Controls. The page lists every key and button.

**The game is too hard, or too easy.** Each level can be played on Story, Normal or Veteran, and you can choose again every time you start a level. The stars you spend in the Codex can be taken back and spent another way at any time.

**How do I start over?** Settings, About, Erase all progress. The game asks twice before it does.
