# Tower and Crown: privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

## The short version

Tower and Crown collects no personal data. There are no ads, no purchases, no analytics and no third-party code in the game. The developer never receives anything about you or about how you play.

## What stays on your device

Your progress (levels won, stars, hero levels, Codex upgrades, settings and your best Survival scores) is saved on your device. A battle you leave half way is saved there too.

## What Apple's services do, if you use them

- **iCloud.** If you are signed in to iCloud and leave "Keep progress in iCloud" on in the game's Settings, your progress is also kept in your own iCloud account so that it follows you to your other devices. It is stored by Apple for you. The developer cannot see it.
- **Game Center.** If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Game Center and shown on leaderboards under your Game Center name. Apple runs Game Center under its own [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). You can play the whole game without it.
- **TestFlight.** If you take part in a beta, Apple gives the developer crash reports and the feedback and screenshots you choose to send through TestFlight.

## Children

The game is suitable for children and collects nothing from anyone, whatever their age.
